What a computer actually does with your code

A computer can't guess what you mean. It reads your instructions one line at a time, top to bottom, and does exactly what each line says.

Analogy. A variable is a labeled box. The label is its name. Whatever you put inside is its value.

What the computer is doing

1 age = 27
age 27
2 name = "Satwik"
name "Satwik"
3 print(name, age)
Satwik 27

Lines 1 and 2 fill two boxes. Line 3 opens both boxes and shows what's inside on the screen.

Predict the output

x = 5
x = x + 2
print(x)

Right. Line 2 takes what's in x (5), adds 2, and stores 7 back in the same box.

Ask anything

Why doesn't x = x + 2 look wrong to Python?
In Python, = means "store", not "is equal to". It works out the right side first (5 + 2 is 7), then puts 7 in the box called x.
